01
The product

The money is in the yard, not the building.

Our launch partner buys industrial parcels whose extra paved yard can be leased for outdoor storage and vehicle parking. Legacy industrial owners rarely price their own parking lot as revenue, so the deals sit in public data that nobody reads at scale. Propelogo reads it for them, then gets them on the phone with the owner.

Today, by hand

One analyst, one city, one spreadsheet

  • Draw yard polygons in Google Earth, one parcel at a time
  • Two zoning lookups per parcel to see if outdoor storage is allowed
  • Re-key everything into a 196-row Excel funnel
  • Hunt for the human behind each LLC, then dial one number at a time
~150–200addresses in one city per month
With Propelogo

One investor covers a state

  • Zoning, use tables, flood, environmental and paved yard ingested nationwide
  • Every parcel scored against five gates and valued automatically
  • Owner lookup finds the person and phone behind the LLC
  • Parallel dialer and bulk personalized email work the list
A stateinstead of a city, at the same headcount

04
The data underneath

Public data, collected county by county.

Every jurisdiction publishes its data differently, so most of the hard work is collection and cleanup. Each parcel gets six data domains. Together they power the five gates and the valuation you saw on the parcel card. Full detail: data categories.

01ParcelsThe lot polygon and IDs. The core record everything attaches to.
02Zoning + use tablesDistrict and the ordinance's permitted uses: is outdoor storage allowed?gate: zoning
03Tax assessorOwner of record, building square feet, year built, assessed value.
04FloodFEMA flood zones (SFHA) per parcel.gate: flood
05EnvironmentalContamination sites on or near the parcel (USTs, brownfields, RCRA).gate: environmental
06Impervious / visionBuilding and paved yard measured from aerial imagery (NAIP) with computer vision.gate: paved yard
Foundation Collected first in every state

Population ≥ 25k or ≥ 5k industrial jobs. ~2,927 jurisdictions, ~79% of the US population.

Expansion Paused

Population 5k–25k or ≥ 1k industrial jobs. Stopped in October so we can finish quality on Foundation.

Completion Deferred

Small, low-industrial places. Waits until national coverage is 50–60%.

Right now: Foundation + Florida QC. Computer vision runs on Florida industrial only. The canonical worklist is 0052 nationwide tiers; QC means checking about 20 parcels per state against reality.

How we work

Spec first. One issue per task. Prove it on staging.

We write the spec and acceptance criteria before anyone builds. Every task is a GitHub issue on the board. Nothing reaches the brokers until someone has clicked it on staging. These three docs answer most "should it do X?" questions.

Acceptance criteria, as checkboxes

  • Callback creates a calendar entry for the chosen time

Calling and the TCPA (US robocall law), in plain words
OKA human calls and, at voicemail, speaks a message.
OKParallel dial, 3–5 lines: the first person who answers gets a human.

Words you'll hear

Parcel
One property lot: its outline plus IDs. Every other piece of data attaches to it.
Corridor · saved view
A ready-made cluster of industrial parcels along a road or in a park. Effectively a call list.
Gates
Five checks a parcel must pass: zoning, residential neighbor, environmental, flood, paved yard.
Engine valuation
Our estimate: building and yard rent capitalized at a market cap rate.
Paved yard · impervious
The hard surface outside the building, measured from aerial imagery. Where the value is.
Owner behind the LLC
The real person who controls an entity owner, found through state registries and lookups.
Skip-trace
A paid lookup for an owner's phone numbers and emails.
Parallel dialing
Ringing several owners at once and connecting the caller to the first who answers.
Foundation tier
The first data sweep in each state: places with ≥ 25k people or ≥ 5k industrial jobs.
Staging
The shared test version of the app, with its own data. Changes are proven here before production.
Campaign
An outreach send, by email or calls, to a set of owners. We never say "project" for this.
Interface
The agreed API or data shape between two pieces of software. "Contract" always means legal.
